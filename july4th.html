July 4th Bike Parade
& Summer Celebration

Saturday, July 4th

A Wyngate Tradition

Bikes, Bands & Fireworks Spirit

Every year on the Fourth of July, Wyngate neighbors gather for our famous Bike Parade and Summer Celebration. Decorate your bike, trike, scooter, or wagon — everyone is welcome — and join a real fire engine as it leads the parade around the neighborhood. Afterward, stick around for a festival with music, games, and treats for the whole family.

Day-Of Schedule

What to Expect

9:30 AM
Bike Decoration Station Opens Streamers, flags, and more — decorate your ride before the parade begins.
10:00 AM
Parade Begins Meet at the corner of Singleton Drive & Bulls Run Parkway — a real fire engine leads the way for one lap around the neighborhood.
~10:20 AM
Festival Kicks Off Live DJ, face painting, games, free popsicles, and the raffle drawing.
12:30 PM
Festival Wraps Up See you next year!

Parade starts at Singleton Drive & Bulls Run Parkway — one lap around the neighborhood.
