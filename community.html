Community Groups

Beyond the WCA, Wyngate neighbors run volunteer groups that look after our trees and look out for one another.

Wyngate Tree Friends

A neighborhood volunteer group working with Montgomery County to restore and protect Wyngate's tree canopy, including help requesting free street trees.

For more information, see wyngatetreefriends.org.

Wyngate & Maplewood Neighbors Helping Neighbors

A joint program connecting neighbors with simple services like rides, errands, and other neighborly help.

For more information, see wmnhn.org.
